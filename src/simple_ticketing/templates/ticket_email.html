How to use your tickets

1. Present on your mobile phone

Simply show the tickets above on your phone at the entrance. Make sure the screen brightness is high enough so the QR code can be scanned properly.

2. Print your tickets

The tickets are also included as a PDF document attached to this email. You can open the PDF file on your computer, tablet, or smartphone and print it. To print the PDF file:

  • Open the attached PDF file using a PDF reader (e.g. Adobe Acrobat Reader).
  • Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Command + P (Mac) to open the print dialog.
  • Select your desired printer.
  • Click Print.
